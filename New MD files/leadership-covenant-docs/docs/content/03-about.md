## Inconsistency to be aware of

The About page names **Ubaid ur Rehman as "CEO, The Leadership Covenant"**, while the Summit page lists him as **"Regional Manager, FranklinCovey Education North Region"**. Both are verbatim from the site. Keep each as-is unless the client says otherwise (logged in `13-OPEN-QUESTIONS.md`).
